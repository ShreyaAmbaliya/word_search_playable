How to play
  • Swipe through letters that share a side. Diagonal steps do not count.
  • A word can be traced forward or backward.
  • Wrong paths simply reset.
  • Tap a word you already found to read its meaning and an example.
  • A hint costs 20 coins and reveals one letter. Tap hint again to reveal the next letter of that same word.
  • Keyboard: arrows move, Enter adds or submits, Backspace undoes, Esc pauses.
